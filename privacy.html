Privacy Policy

Last updated November 11, 2026

The Order of The Shadow Hook (“Shadow Hook”, “we”) runs this app for casual longshore workers. This page explains what the app collects, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

  • Account details you give us. Your name as printed on your dispatch card, your dispatch number, and the handle you choose. We use the card details to confirm you are a casual worker.
  • What you put in the app. Dockets and job entries, photos you attach, posts, comments, messages, board marks and job reports.
  • Optional details. Whether you are a veteran or a Gold Star family member, and any name you add to the remembrance wall, only if you choose to share them.
  • Location, only when you use it. If you allow it, the app reads your phone’s location in two places: to tag a post with the terminal you’re nearest to (only the terminal’s name is saved, not where you were standing), and when you tap “I’m at the gate” on a gate check, which adds your exact position to that message. The app doesn’t track your location in the background.
  • App activity. The days you open the app, and basic device and error information, used to keep the app working and to find problems.
  • Messages to support. If you write to us through the Support page, we keep your name, email address and message so we can reply.

What we don’t do

We don’t sell your information. We don’t show ads. We don’t share your information with employers, the PMA, or the union.

Who can see what

  • Your handle, posts, comments, board marks and job reports are visible to other signed-in members.
  • Your name, dispatch number, dockets, pay figures and messages are private to you. The people who run the app can see account details when they need to verify a member or handle a report.
  • If a post, comment or member is reported, the moderators can see the reported content.

Where it’s kept

Your data is stored with our hosting provider, Supabase, in the United States, and is sent over encrypted connections.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data for as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, your account and the data tied to it are removed. Posts you made on the shared board are removed with it.

Your choices

  • Edit or remove your posts and entries at any time.
  • Block or report another member.
  • Delete your account and its data in the app: Profile, then Delete. If you can’t get into the app, ask us through the Support page and we’ll do it for you.
  • Ask what we hold about you, or ask us to correct it, through the Support page.

Children

The app is for adult workers and is not directed to anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update the date at the top and tell members in the app.

Contact

Questions about privacy go through the Support page.
