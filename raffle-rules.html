Official Raffle Rules

Effective November 18, 2026 · run by The Order of The Shadow Hook LLC
The short version
It's free
No purchase necessary. Tickets can't be bought, with money or with Port Bucks (⬡). You earn them by using the app.
How you win
Each ticket is one chance. One ticket is drawn at random on the server after entries close.
Apple and Google aren't involved
Apple and Google are not sponsors of these raffles and are not involved in them in any way.

These rules cover every raffle run in The Shadow Hook app and at theshadowhook.com. The prize, the dates and the ticket limit for each raffle are shown on that raffle's card in the app and are part of these rules.

1 · Who runs it

The raffles are run by The Order of The Shadow Hook LLC ("we"), Los Angeles County, California. Questions go to support@theshadowhook.com.

Apple and Google are not sponsors. Apple Inc. and Google LLC are not sponsors of these raffles, do not endorse them, and are not involved in them in any way. Prize brands (for example a show, a team or a venue) belong to their owners, who are not sponsors either unless the raffle card says so.

2 · Who can enter

  • You must be at least 18 and a legal resident of the United States.
  • You need a member account in good standing. One account per person; tickets on extra or shared accounts are void.
  • The Keymaster, the owners of The Order of The Shadow Hook LLC, and the people who live in their households can't win.
  • Void where prohibited.

3 · How to get tickets

No purchase necessary. Buying anything does not improve your chances. Tickets are earned for things anyone can do for free in the app:

  • Open the app 5 days in a row.
  • Open it every day of a week (Monday to Sunday).
  • Mark the board 5 times during one dispatch.
  • Report 3 job drops on one board.

From time to time we also hand out bonus tickets (for example for veterans, or for bringing in a new member). When we do, we say so in the app.

Limits

  • You can earn up to 6 tickets a week from the list above (weeks run Monday to Sunday, Pacific time). Bonus tickets don't count toward the 6.
  • Each raffle may cap how many tickets one member can hold; the cap is on the raffle card.
  • Tickets go into the raffle that's running. Tickets earned when no raffle is running, or after you hit a raffle's cap, are kept for the next raffle.
  • Tickets have no cash value and can't be sold, traded or transferred.

Marks and job drops have to be real. Tickets earned with false reports, extra accounts, scripts or anything else that games the system are void, and the account can be removed from the raffle or closed.

4 · The draw

Entries close at the time on the raffle card. After that, one ticket is picked at random by the server from every valid ticket in that raffle. Each ticket has the same chance. Your odds depend on how many tickets you hold and how many are in the raffle in total; the app shows your odds as you go.

If a raffle is called off before the draw, we'll say so in the app. Tickets in it go back to their holders for the next raffle.

5 · Winning

  • The winner's handle is posted on the raffle card and the banner in the app, and we contact the winner through the app.
  • The winner has 7 days to reply. If they don't, or can't meet these rules, a new ticket is drawn the same way.
  • We may ask the winner to confirm their identity and age before handing over the prize.
  • The prize is what's described on the raffle card. It can't be swapped for cash. If a prize becomes unavailable, we may substitute one of equal or greater value.
  • Taxes on a prize are the winner's. If a prize is worth $600 or more, we'll ask for a W-9 and send a 1099, as the IRS requires.
  • Prizes that involve an event or travel follow that event's own rules, dates and terms.

6 · The rest

We can cancel, change or pause a raffle if something goes wrong that we can't fix fairly (a bug, fraud, or anything outside our control). If we change a raffle's dates or prize, we'll say so on its card.

By entering, you agree to these rules and to our decisions, which are final. To the extent the law allows, we're not responsible for technical problems, lost entries, or anything that happens with a prize after it's handed over. Your information is handled as described in our Privacy Policy, and our Terms of Service also apply.

These rules are governed by the laws of the State of California. Any dispute belongs in the state or federal courts sitting in Los Angeles County, California.

To find out who won a raffle, look at the raffle card in the app or email support@theshadowhook.com within 30 days of the draw.
