Support

Shadow Hook is a members’ app for casual longshore workers. If something isn’t working, or you have a question about your account, this is the place.

Common questions

I can’t sign in. Enter your name exactly as it’s printed on your dispatch card, and your dispatch number with its letter (for example D4928). If it still won’t let you in, write to us below with the name and number you’re using.

How do I delete my account? In the app, open your Profile and choose Delete. That removes your account and its data. If you can’t get into the app, write to us below and we’ll delete it for you.

How do I report a post or a member? Tap the flag on any post or comment, or open the member’s profile and tap Report. To stop seeing someone, tap Block on their profile. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours.

The numbers look wrong. Dispatch counts and the Wire’s call come from the hall’s posted sheets and from members’ reports. Tell us which board and what you saw, and we’ll check it.

Who runs this? The app is run by members. It is not an official ILWU or PMA service.
