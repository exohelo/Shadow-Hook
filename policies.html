1 · What this app is

The Shadow Hook is a tool for casual longshore workers: a docket scanner and job log, a dispatch-board predictor called the Wire, a record of the hall's boards, chat rooms, and a gear shop called the Armory.

It is an independent app. It is not operated by, endorsed by, or affiliated with the ILWU, ILWU Local 13, the PMA, any terminal operator, or any employer. Nothing here is an official dispatch record, and nothing here changes what the hall or your employer says.

The Wire is an estimate, not a promise. Every prediction, percentage, forecast and alert in this app is a guess built from past boards and the numbers members feed in. It is often wrong. Do not treat it as a guarantee of work, and do not make a decision you cannot afford to be wrong about — driving to the hall, skipping another job, turning down a shift — on the strength of a number on this screen. We do not compensate for a missed job, a wasted trip, or a prediction that did not come true.

6 · Your data, briefly

Your job log, hours, balance and profile are stored with our backend provider (Supabase) so the same account works on every device you sign in on. Docket photos stay on the phone that scanned them — the job log that syncs carries only a note that a photo exists, not the picture. That also means we cannot recover a photo from a phone you have lost or wiped.

The one exception is a correction you send us on purpose: when you report a wrong terminal and attach the slip as proof, that photo is uploaded with your report so the Keymaster can check it. It is kept with the report and used for nothing else.

We do not sell your data. We do not share your job log with employers, the hall, or the union. Aggregate, de-identified numbers — counts and board movements with no name on them — are what the Wire's predictions are built from, and those are visible to other members by design.
