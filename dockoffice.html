BECOME AN AMBASSADOR

The Order pays the hands who bring makers to the Armory.

What it is

An ambassador is a recruiter. You know a print shop, a leather worker, a sticker maker, somebody with gear the docks would actually wear — you bring them to the Order. Their goods get checked, the worthy gear gets shelved, and it sells right here in the Armory.

How it works
1 · Apply belowEvery application gets read, and word comes back either way.
2 · Get crownedThis deck opens up — your own gate for signing up vendors and watching your money.
3 · Get paid on their salesEvery sale off your recruits’ benches pays you a cut of the house margin — booked automatically at the till, on every sale, while you hold your crown and their shop is with the Order. You don’t touch inventory, shipping, or money handling.
4 · Nothing out of pocketHow you’re paid gets sorted once you’re crowned. No buy-in, no fees, nothing owed by you.

The Order sets the commission rate and the terms of the arrangement and may change them, with word to you first. This is an invitation to apply, not a contract, and nothing here is a promise of future earnings.
