BECOME AN AMBASSADOR

The Order pays the hands who bring makers to the Armory.

What it is

An ambassador is a recruiter. You know a print shop, a leather worker, a sticker maker, somebody with gear the docks would actually wear — you bring them to the Order. Their goods get checked, the worthy gear gets shelved, and it sells right here in the Armory.

How it works
1 · Apply belowEvery application gets read, and word comes back either way.
2 · Get crownedThis deck opens up — your own gate for signing up vendors and watching your money.
3 · Get paid, foreverEvery sale off your recruits’ benches pays you a cut of the house margin — booked automatically at the till, on every sale, for as long as they sell. You don’t touch inventory, shipping, or money handling.
4 · Nothing out of pocketHow you’re paid gets sorted once you’re crowned. Nothing comes out of your pocket. Ever.
