LOAD THE SHIP AGAINST THE CLOCK · KEEP HER UPRIGHT · DON'T GET FIRED

Tap a column and the crane drops the box in it. Every box has a weight: 10t, 20t or 30t HEAVY.
Load one side too heavy and she lists. Stow heavies up high and she gets top-heavy. Load past the load line and she sinks. Any of those and you're fired.
Can't fit a heavy? Shut it out (+2s). Finish level, too: every degree of list left at the end costs time.

Same boxes for everyone today, so the board is fair.
